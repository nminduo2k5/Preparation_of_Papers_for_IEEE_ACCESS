\noindent\textbf{Conclusion:} The experimental results indicate that LLM performance in stock forecasting is highly dependent on the prediction horizon and market structure. While GPT-4o demonstrates strong accuracy in short-term forecasting, particularly for volatile stocks, Google Gemini 2.0 exhibits greater suitability for medium- and long-term predictions, achieving more stable error behavior and consistent performance across different coordination architectures. These characteristics suggest that Gemini 2.0 is better aligned with trend-oriented and strategic forecasting applications, whereas short-term accuracy-driven tasks may benefit more from GPT-4o.
